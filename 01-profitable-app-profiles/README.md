# Profitable App Profiles for the App Store and Google Play Markets

## Objective

Analyzed mobile app data from the App Store and Google Play to identify app profiles likely to attract users for a company building free, ad-supported apps.

## Key Findings

- **Books and Reference** apps show strong potential for user engagement on both platforms
- Games and entertainment apps dominate in popularity but face high competition
- Recommended a hybrid approach: a book app with interactive features (quizzes, mini-games) to combine high engagement with lower competition

## Methodology

- Cleaned datasets by removing duplicates, handling missing values, and filtering to English-language free apps
- Built frequency tables to analyze app distribution across genres
- Calculated average user counts by genre to identify high-engagement categories

## Data

- [Google Play Store Apps](https://www.kaggle.com/lava18/google-play-store-apps) — ~10,000 apps
- [Apple App Store Apps](https://www.kaggle.com/ramamet4/app-store-apple-data-set-10k-apps) — ~7,000 apps
